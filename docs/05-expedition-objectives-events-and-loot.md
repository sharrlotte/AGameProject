# Expedition Objectives, Random Events & Loot

## 1. Meaningful Mission Objectives
Procedural maps generate **1 Primary Objective** + **1–2 Optional Side Objectives** per expedition so every run has direction beyond "walk around and loot."

| Objective Type | Core Gameplay Loop | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Supply Recovery** | Locate a marked drop/shipment crate in a dangerous zone, break/unlock it, and extract the heavy cargo item. | Recover 2 Bio-Containment Cases from the Highway Military Convoy. |
| **Power Restoration** | Find fuel/breaker switches across a district or building to restore power—unlocking restricted doors or activating an extraction gate. | Restore the Hospital backup generator to unlock the Restricted Research wing. |
| **Data Recovery** | Reach a server/terminal in the Underground Lab or City Center, initiate a noisy download (mini-holdout), and extract the drive. | Download vaccine research from Sub-Level B while defending for 30s. |
| **Rescue** | Locate trapped survivor(s), clear immediate threats, and escort them alive to an extraction point. | Rescue a trapped scientist from the City Pharmacy (follows player, can take cover). |
| **Bounty** | Track and eliminate a named Elite/Mutated Zombie guarding a territory for rare tech rewards. | Hunt *"The Butcher"* (armored juggernaut Tank) inside the Industrial Foundry. |
| **Signal Investigation** | Follow directional audio/HUD pings to triangulate an unknown beacon—could lead to hidden military caches or an ambush. | Trace a repeating distress pulse into the foggy Suburbs. |
| **Emergency Defense** | Activate and defend a temporary automated outpost/relay against timed waves for bonus supply drops. | Hold the Street Barricade relay for 60s to earn an orbital/airdrop cache. |

## 2. Dynamic Random Events
Events trigger dynamically based on escalation tier, noise, or proximity to interrupt routine looting:

| Random Event | Trigger & Immediate Impact | Player Decision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Supply Helicopter Crash** | Loud distant boom + smoke column visible across chunks; draws nearby zombies toward the crash site. | Race to loot high-tier military gear before the horde swarms it, or ignore it to stay safe. |
| **Abandoned Military Convoy** | Spawns along Highway/City avenues with armored vehicles and locked weapon crates; guarded by Tanks/Runners. | Worth spending grenades/heavy ammo to crack open? |
| **Building Alarm Activates** | Triggered by breaching a locked pharmacy/bank door or stray explosion; flashes red lights and continuously pulls zombies within 80m. | Grab the best loot fast and sprint out, or cut the alarm box first. |
| **Survivor Requests Assistance** | Radio distress call nearby; short timer before they are overrun. | Detour to save them for base personnel/reputation rewards, or prioritize your main objective. |
| **Dangerous Horde Migration** | Massive pack (Walkers + Runners + 1–2 Specials) sweeps across a district along a visible path. | Duck inside a quiet building, turn off flashlight/hold fire, or set up a mine/turret ambush. |

## 3. Data-Driven Loot & Inventory System

### 3.1 Loot Categories
- **Weapons & Attachments:** Complete guns (rare) and modular weapon parts (optics, muzzles, mags, cores).
- **Crafting & Base Resources:** Scrap Metal, Electronics, Chemicals, Medical Supplies, Rare Research Tech.
- **Consumables:** Bandages, Medkits, Stamina Stims, Ammo Boxes, Grenades.
- **Deployable Charges / Salvage:** Turret kits, Mine packs, Drone batteries.
- **Valuables / Mission Items:** Encrypted drives, specimen jars, keycards.

### 3.2 Containers & Biome Weighting
Containers are spawned according to room tags (`Pharmacy_Cabinet`, `Military_Crate`, `Lab_Server`, `House_Fridge`, `Industrial_Locker`) with **search duration** (e.g., 1.5s progress ring, interrupted if you move/shoot) so looting in unsafe rooms creates vulnerability.

### 3.3 Backpack / Loadout Tension
- **Weight & Slot Limits:** You cannot loot everything. Carrying too much weight slows movement speed or increases sprint stamina drain—forcing tough drop decisions ("Drop scrap metal to carry the rare Lab Core?").
- **Quick-Access Belt vs. Backpack:** Weapons, meds, and deployables sit on hotkeys; scavenged junk sits in the backpack and only converts into permanent base resources upon **successful extraction**.
