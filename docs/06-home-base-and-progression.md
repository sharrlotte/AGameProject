# Home Base & Progression Hub

## 1. Overview
Outside the quarantine zones, the player operates from a **Home Base**—the permanent progression hub. Because dying on an expedition loses run loot while **keeping base progress**, every successful extraction permanently strengthens future runs.

*(Can be presented either as a walkable 3D safe-house hub or fast-navigable tactical base view—walkable hub with quick-tab shortcuts gives the best of both immersion and speed.)*

---

## 2. The 7 Base Facilities

| Facility | Gameplay Function | Upgrade Progression Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Workshop** | Weapon crafting, attachment fabrication, weapon repairs/stat tuning, and crafting mines/turrets. | Lv1: Craft basic ammo & Gun Turrets.<br>Lv2: Craft Suppressors, Mags & Electric Traps.<br>Lv3: Craft Heavy Turrets & high-tier weapons. |
| **2. Armory** | Weapon/gear storage stash, saved **Loadout Presets**, and pre-expedition equipping. | Increases stash capacity and unlocks extra saved loadout slots (e.g., *"Stealth Lab Run"* vs *"Heavy Horde Clear"*). |
| **3. Laboratory** | Research new technologies, analyze Rare Lab Tech extracted from zones, and unlock passive perks/ability upgrades. | Unlocks elemental ammo cores, better armor plating, faster extraction beacons, and Stalker counters. |
| **4. Drone Station** | Unlock, upgrade, and customize Scout & Combat Drones (battery life, scan radius, drone weapon type). | Lv1: Basic Scout Drone.<br>Lv2: Combat Drone unlock.<br>Lv3: Dual-module drones (e.g., Scout + Ping Weakpoints). |
| **5. Medical Room** | Post-expedition recovery, crafting advanced Medkits/Stims, and permanent health/stamina conditioning upgrades. | Increases starting max HP, stamina regen rate, and crafts Combat Stims. |
| **6. Radio Room** | Scans and unlocks new quarantine zone sectors, higher-risk/higher-reward regions, and special bounty contracts. | Reveals region modifiers before deploy (e.g., *"High Bio-Hazard / +40% Lab Tech"*) and lets you choose insertion regions. |
| **7. Generator** | Supplies **Power Budget** to all other base facilities. | Upgrading the Generator increases max wattage so you can run higher-level Workshop + Lab + Drone Station simultaneously. |

---

## 3. Generator Power Budget Mechanic (Strategic Base Choice)
Instead of linearly maxing everything mindlessly, the **Generator** acts as a strategic bottleneck:
- Each facility level consumes Power (e.g., `Workshop Lv3 = 4 Power`, `Drone Station Lv2 = 3 Power`).
- Early on, if your Generator produces `8 Power`, you might toggle facilities on/off between runs:
  - *Need a new rifle?* Power the Workshop & Armory.
  - *Preparing for Underground Lab?* Divert power to the Drone Station & Medical Room.
- Extracting rare **Industrial Generators / Fuel Cells** lets you expand total base power permanently.

## 4. Progression & Loadout Loop Example
1. **Run #1 (Starter):** Deploy to Suburbs with basic Pistol + SMG. Extract with Scrap, Electronics, and 1 Medical Cache.
2. **Base Upgrade:** Upgrade Workshop (Lv1 -> Lv2) and craft a **Pump Shotgun** + **Proximity Mines**.
3. **Run #2 (Hospital):** Survive Runners using Shotgun + Mines, recover **Research Data** + **Rare Lab Tech**.
4. **Base Upgrade:** Spend Lab Tech in the Laboratory to unlock **Pulse Scan** ability and upgrade **Scout Drone** to tag Stalkers.
5. **Run #3 (Underground Lab / Military):** Enter high-threat zones equipped for armor, darkness, and extraction sieges.
